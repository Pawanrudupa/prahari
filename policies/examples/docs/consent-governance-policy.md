# Section 1 Scope and Purpose
This Consent Management and Purpose Limitation Standard outlines the requirements for recording customer consent artifacts, enforcing processing boundaries, and managing data subject withdrawal requests across automated workflows.

# Section 2 Notice and Purpose Specification
## Section 2.1 Explicit Purpose Binding
Customer personal data collected for a specified transaction or support inquiry must never be processed for secondary purposes—such as general model fine-tuning or cross-product marketing—without distinct and verifiable consent.

## Section 2.2 Granular Consent Categories
Consent records must articulate the exact scope of permissible processing:
- Account maintenance and billing communications.
- Fraud monitoring and risk analytics.
- Automated assistant responses and personalized query resolution.

# Section 3 Consent Lifecycle and Revocation
## Section 3.1 Unconditional Right of Withdrawal
Customers may withdraw consent for optional processing categories at any time through self-service interfaces. Upon receipt of a withdrawal signal, agents must cease accessing the corresponding data attributes within 15 minutes.

## Section 3.2 Post-Withdrawal Erasure Obligations
When consent is revoked, historical records not required for statutory audit or financial reconciliation must be marked for deletion or anonymization, removing all identifiable linkages.

# Section 4 Audit Trail and Verifiable Proof
## Section 4.1 Immutable Consent Logging
Every consent grant, renewal, modification, and revocation event must be committed to the append-only, hash-chained audit log with cryptographic sequencing to ensure evidentiary integrity.

## Section 4.2 Cross-Border Processing Safeguards
Agent workflows transferring personal records across geographical boundary zones must verify that destination storage environments comply with equivalent encryption, residency, and retention standards.
